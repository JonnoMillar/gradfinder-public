I'm in my final year of Economics at the University of Bristol, predicted a 2:1. I use Python and SQL for my
dissertation on regional house prices, and Tableau for anything that needs to be read by people who don't write SQL.

[R1 or R2 below, then:] [ROLE-SPECIFIC: one sourced fact about this firm, team or programme, and how it connects.]
