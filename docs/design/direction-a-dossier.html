Ready to shape the future of energy through data and technology? At EDF, Success is Personal. You’ll learn from experts, build valuable technical skills and start a career that helps Britain achieve net zero.

The opportunity

Alongside earning a salary of £35,000 per annum, you’ll join our two-year Data and Technology Graduate Programme, expected to begin in September 2027. From day one, you’ll become part of our graduate community and start building a career that combines technology, innovation and purpose.

Over the course of the programme, you’ll complete placements across different areas of our Data and Technology function, gaining experience in Software Engineering, Data Science and AI, Data Engineering and Cloud Computing. As you develop your skills, you’ll contribute to projects that help transform EDF and support Britain’s transition to a low-carbon future.

You’ll enjoy a flexible working experience, with most of your time spent working remotely or from an EDF office, whichever suits you best. You’ll also have opportunities to spend time at sites and offices across EDF, including locations such as London, Brighton and Exeter. This is a great way to experience different parts of the business, build your network and see first-hand how technology supports the delivery of cleaner energy.

What you’ll experience

No two placements are the same. You’ll work alongside technology specialists, data scientists, software engineers and business teams while developing skills that are in demand across every industry.
